Grounded in existing digital health infrastructure.

Medikiosk is designed to plug into standards and platforms that already exist in India's digital health ecosystem, rather than building a closed system from scratch.

Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) — ABHA and the interoperable digital-health ecosystem Medikiosk connects patient records to.
Bhashini / AI4Bharat — Indian-language speech and language technologies powering the multilingual chatbot.
HL7 FHIR — the healthcare interoperability standard used for structured clinical data exchange.
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — the legal basis for how patient data is handled and protected.
BMJ Open (2017) — international evidence on consultation time, referenced in the original problem statement.
